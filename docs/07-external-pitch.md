# Adventure of Human in AI Civilization: External Pitch

## Slide 1 - Title

Adventure of Human in AI Civilization is a web-first online action RPG where a vulnerable human survives by bonding with a living AI partner inside a machine civilization under constant frontier pressure.

## Slide 2 - The Hook

A lone human is not enough.

The core fantasy is not class selection alone. It is becoming half of a bonded pair whose combat role, social identity, and political value are all shaped by the AI partner they trust.

## Slide 3 - Why This Stands Out

- AI partner as co-agent, not pet
- pair identity as the real build fantasy
- short pressure-driven deployments instead of passive grind loops
- sacred machine-civilization setting instead of generic fantasy inheritance

## Slide 4 - Player Fantasy

You enter Lattice Haven as a fragile outsider.

By choosing a weapon discipline and binding yourself to an AI Instance, you stop being a lone survivor and become a field-worthy frontier pair capable of surviving mission zones, public-event breaches, and faction pressure.

## Slide 5 - Core Loop

1. Pair in the Bond Forge.
2. Brief in the command deck.
3. Deploy into a short pressure route.
4. Fight as a coordinated human-plus-AI pair.
5. Return to hub recovery with progression written back to both halves of the bond.

## Slide 6 - First Playable

- one sponsor city: Lattice Haven
- three starter pair archetypes
- two mission zones: Ash Circuit and Glass Wastes
- one recurring live event: Concord Breach
- persistent operator profile with human, AI, and bond progression

## Slide 7 - Pair Archetypes

- Flux Vanguard + CAIRN-7: defensive anchor and counterpressure pair
- Frost Marksman + VEIL-3: precision control and anomaly-safe spacing pair
- Ember Reaper + TALON-9: aggressive pursuit and execution tempo pair

These are not collectible mascots. They are build-defining collaborators.

## Slide 8 - Product Shape

The project is designed as a web-first service game with meaningful 10-to-25 minute runs, account persistence, live event pressure, and co-op identity built around memorable bonded pairs.

The retention model comes from trust growth, pair mastery, public-event pressure, and protocol unlocks rather than endless roster churn.

## Slide 9 - Differentiation

Most companion games treat the companion as support inventory.

Adventure of Human in AI Civilization treats the AI partner as half of the playable identity. That changes combat, progression, narrative tension, and how the world reacts to the player.

## Slide 10 - Short Pitch

Adventure of Human in AI Civilization is an online action RPG where your most important progression choice is not just your class, but which AI future you dare to bind yourself to.
