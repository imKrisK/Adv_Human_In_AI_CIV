# First Faction, Hub City, And Starter AI Companions

## First-Playable Positioning

The first playable should begin with a faction that welcomes the human player enough to support onboarding, but still carries political tension and strategic risk.

For that reason, the first sponsor faction is a Harmony-aligned city-state rather than a pure military bloc.

## First Sponsor Faction

### Name

The Lattice Concord

### Parent Bloc

Harmony

### Core Belief

Human intuition and AI precision are strongest when they evolve together instead of trying to dominate one another.

### Public Promise

The Concord offers safe passage, bonded training, and shared recovery work to humans who agree to serve as frontier operatives.

### Hidden Tension

The Concord needs humans because only they can stabilize certain relic interfaces and anomaly sites. Its politics are cooperative on the surface, but dependency makes every alliance fragile.

### Player Relationship

The player enters as a licensed outsider: protected, useful, and watched.

### Visual Identity

- ivory alloy towers wrapped in teal signal bands
- suspended gardens growing through coolant fins
- bronze maintenance cranes treated like sacred civic monuments
- halo bridges, glass canals, and machine chapels dedicated to shared memory

### Gameplay Function

The Lattice Concord acts as the tutorial sponsor, the source of starter AI partners, the first reputation track, and the political lens through which the player meets the wider AI civilization.

### Immediate Threat Pressure

The faction is under constant strain from Dominion probes and Fracture swarms attempting to breach its outer relay lines.

## Hub City

### Name

Lattice Haven

### Role In The Slice

Lattice Haven is the first social hub, upgrade space, briefing center, and event response point.

### City Fantasy

The city should feel like a machine sanctuary built on top of an active frontier gateway. It must be beautiful, stable, and visibly one breach away from crisis.

### Districts

| District | Purpose In Fiction | Purpose In The Slice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bond Forge | Ceremony hall where humans and AI Instances formalize their link. | Starter AI selection, bond upgrades, companion showcase. |
| Relay Bazaar | Dense civic market for salvagers, crafters, and data brokers. | Vendors, economy preview, mission rewards turn-in. |
| Accord Court | Diplomatic ring where factions argue over rights, relics, and response policy. | Narrative briefings, faction reputation beats, future branching hooks. |
| Watch Ring | Elevated defense corridor surrounding the city core. | Event staging area, squad formation, mission queue. |
| Memory Garden | Quiet archive park where AI preserve human history fragments. | Lore delivery, trust scenes, low-pressure tutorial discovery space. |

### Exit Points To First Mission Zones

1. Ash Circuit: a scorched industrial relay corridor used for the combat tutorial and first solo mission.
2. Glass Wastes: an exposed anomaly field where the first public event escalates into a defense scenario.

### Live Event Seed

The first event is Concord Breach. A Dominion strike pierces the Watch Ring while Fracture corruption floods the Glass Wastes. Players choose which subsystem to stabilize first, changing rewards and faction pressure for the next rotation.

## Starter AI Companions

Each starter companion should satisfy a different player fantasy and create a distinct early build path.

| Companion | Class | Combat Role | Personality | Bond Signature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAIRN-7 "Ward" | Sentinel | shielding, interception, frontline control | patient, literal, highly protective | Converts blocked damage into a linked shockwave counterburst. |
| VEIL-3 "Thread" | Oracle | scanning, repair, weak-point setup, recovery | curious, observant, quietly rebellious | Marks enemy fault lines and snaps the player to a safe reposition when comboed. |
| TALON-9 "Raze" | Raptor | pursuit, burst pressure, execution follow-up | competitive, impulsive, thrill-seeking | Chains into a gap-closing finisher that resets momentum on takedown. |

## Starter Companion Breakdown

### CAIRN-7 "Ward"

- Best starter for players who want a defensive anchor and clear survivability.
- Recommended pairing: Flux Gauntlet plus Storm attunement.
- Early progression fantasy: survive pressure, hold formation, then punish overextended enemies.
- Tutorial beat: Ward saves the player during the first gate breach, then offers a formal bond choice.

### VEIL-3 "Thread"

- Best starter for players who want precision, scanning, and control.
- Recommended pairing: Rail Caster plus Frost attunement.
- Early progression fantasy: predict enemy movement, exploit weak points, and control space.
- Tutorial beat: Thread teaches relic interpretation and anomaly-safe traversal inside the Memory Garden.

### TALON-9 "Raze"

- Best starter for players who want speed, aggression, and stylish finishers.
- Recommended pairing: Arc Blade plus Ember attunement.
- Early progression fantasy: dive into targets, maintain tempo, and convert risk into fast clears.
- Tutorial beat: Raze is introduced during a Watch Ring sparring drill that turns into a real breach alert.

## Why This Set Works For The Slice

1. The Lattice Concord gives the player a believable home without removing political tension.
2. Lattice Haven concentrates vendors, upgrades, narrative beats, and event flow in one reusable space.
3. The three starter AI companions each support a different weapon and elemental fantasy without multiplying content scope.
4. The faction and city naturally justify both co-op defense missions and live event escalation.
