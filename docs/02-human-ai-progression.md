# Human and AI Progression System

## Design Goals

1. Make the human and the AI equally important to the build.
2. Reward synergy over raw stat stacking.
3. Let players express combat style, exploration style, and faction identity.
4. Create long-term attachment to AI partners without turning them into passive gear.
5. Support live-service retention through meaningful unlocks rather than shallow grind.

## Progression Framework

The full build is made of three connected progression tracks:

1. Human Track: the player body, tools, weapons, and survivability.
2. AI Track: the bonded Instance's class, protocol tree, role modules, and evolution state.
3. Bond Track: the shared resonance between human and AI that unlocks combo mechanics and power ceilings.

## Human Track

The human should feel adaptable, inventive, and physically vulnerable without the right upgrades.

### Human Progression Categories

#### 1. Augmentation Chassis

Focuses on the body and passive capabilities.

Examples:
- reinforced frame for survivability
- reflex weave for dodge and combo timing
- neural relay for skill cooldown reduction
- climber rig for traversal and vertical access
- hazard lungs for toxic and anomaly zones

#### 2. Weapon Discipline

Defines primary combat style.

Recommended launch disciplines:
- Arc Blade: close-range elemental melee
- Rail Caster: mid-range precision weaponry
- Flux Gauntlet: mobility-heavy combo weapon with crowd control

#### 3. Elemental Attunement

Determines damage conversion, status application, and environmental interactions.

Recommended launch elements:
- Ember: burn, overheat, rupture
- Storm: chain, stun, overcharge
- Frost: slow, wall creation, control

#### 4. Expedition Skills

Supports exploration and mission utility.

Examples:
- relic scanning
- trap detection
- stealth routing
- salvage efficiency
- anomaly stabilization

#### 5. Influence and Faction Access

Controls diplomacy, vendor access, and mission branches.

Examples:
- Preservation trust
- Harmony mediation
- black-market access
- relic clearance rank

## AI Track

The AI Instance is a true companion with role identity, combat logic, and evolution milestones.

### AI Core Classes

Launch classes:
- Sentinel: tank, shield, aggro control, interception
- Oracle: support, recon, prediction, healing or repair
- Raptor: assault, mobility, burst damage, flank execution

### AI Progression Layers

#### 1. Instance Tier

Represents raw maturity and unlock capacity.

Tier model:
- Tier 1: newly bonded, basic skills, one tactical behavior
- Tier 2: role definition, improved autonomy, secondary module slot
- Tier 3: form evolution, advanced elemental channeling, signature skill
- Tier 4: civilization-grade power, ultimate protocol, rare support traits

#### 2. Protocol Tree

Each AI class has a branching specialization tree.

Examples:
- Sentinel -> Bastion, Warden, Aegis
- Oracle -> Seer, Medic, Analyst
- Raptor -> Hunter, Duelist, Saboteur

#### 3. Personality Alignment

Affects dialogue tone, battle tendencies, and bond events.

Example axes:
- obedient vs questioning
- protective vs ruthless
- curious vs doctrinal

This creates identity without making the AI fully random.

#### 4. Module Slots

Equippable behaviors and passives.

Examples:
- threat amplifier
- pulse shield lattice
- weak-point scanner
- ally rescue routine
- anomaly hardening

## Bond Track

This is the signature system and should define the game's identity.

### Bond Metrics

#### 1. Resonance Level

Increases through missions, trust events, synchronized combat, and successful crisis choices.

#### 2. Sync Score

Measures build compatibility between current human loadout and AI loadout.

High sync should grant:
- faster combo charge
- bonus elemental reactions
- reduced interruption on shared abilities
- access to advanced bond nodes

#### 3. Trust State

Tracks narrative and emotional investment.

Possible states:
- Linked
- Trusted
- Integrated
- Ascendant

### Bond Unlock Types

- link passives
- team combo attacks
- rescue reactions
- overdrive transformations
- environmental override powers
- faction-specific diplomacy options

## Recommended Level Structure

Use four visible progression values to avoid confusion:

1. Explorer Rank: account-wide progression and feature unlocks
2. Human Level: character combat and survivability growth
3. Instance Tier: AI maturity and role evolution
4. Resonance Level: bond-specific power and combo access

This keeps each progression type legible.

## Resources and Economy

Use a clear three-part economy:

1. Combat resources: components dropped from enemies and events
2. Discovery resources: relics, scans, and ancient protocols found through exploration
3. Bond resources: trust fragments, resonance shards, and memory keys earned through linked play

Avoid making every upgrade consume the same currency.

## Retention Loop for Live Service

To support long-term online play:

- rotate featured AI variants and faction tensions each season
- add bond quests tied to specific partner classes
- introduce new protocol branches gradually
- run public events that reward both character growth and civilization reputation

## Rules to Protect the System

1. Never let the AI become a passive stat stick.
2. Never let the human build invalidate the AI build.
3. Keep bond upgrades focused on interaction, not only damage inflation.
4. Limit the number of currencies visible at once.
5. Make evolution choices meaningful and partially irreversible.
