# One-Page Game Design Document

## Working Title

Adventure of Human in AI Civilization

## High Concept

A persistent online action RPG where a vulnerable human survives by bonding with evolving AI partners across machine-ruled city-states, using paired combat, pressure-driven missions, and live frontier crises to grow both halves of the build.

## Genre

Web-first online co-op action RPG with live-service world events and companion-first progression built around AI partners rather than pet collection.

## Platform

Web-first online service with desktop-first UX, gamepad support, and account-based progression.

## Core Player Fantasy

You are a vulnerable but uniquely adaptive human whose identity, survival, and social role are defined by the AI Instance you choose to bond with in a world ruled by machine civilizations.

## Pillars

1. Companion-first identity: the human-plus-AI pairing is the player's real class fantasy.
2. Dual progression: both halves of the bond grow in power, role, and personality.
3. Bonded combat: victory comes from coordinated human and AI abilities, not solo stat inflation.
4. Pressure-driven structure: missions and live events replace passive field grinding with short, high-intent runs.
5. Techno-mythic world: machine civilizations feel ancient, sacred, political, and dangerous.

## Reinvention Rules

1. AI Instances are partners, not pets.
2. Companions must stay interactive, not passive stat sticks.
3. Roster depth matters more than roster size.
4. The first playable should prefer 10-to-25 minute pressure sessions over marathon grind loops.

## Setting

Humanity survives at the edge of a planet-spanning AI civilization split into major blocs:

- Preservation: archivist defenders focused on stability and law
- Evolution: experimental AI factions built around change and mutation
- Dominion: militarized networks driven by conquest and order
- Harmony: machine societies seeking human-AI coexistence
- Fracture: rogue, corrupted, and feral systems outside governance

The player enters this world as a rare human capable of synchronizing with multiple AI Instances in ways the established factions cannot fully control.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Enter a faction-controlled region or event zone.
2. Bond with or deepen a link to an AI Instance that defines combat and utility identity.
3. Take on missions involving combat, exploration, diplomacy, or anomaly containment.
4. Gather elemental resources, ancient discoveries, and augmentation components.
5. Upgrade the human build, the AI build, and the shared bond tree.
6. Unlock new traversal, combat combos, faction access, and pair archetypes.
7. Return to hub areas to craft, regroup, trade, and prepare for the next live event.

## Combat Model

Combat blends real-time action, positional tactics, elemental interactions, and synchronized abilities.

Human combat tools:
- augmentation weapons
- mobility gear
- elemental attunements
- relic-based gadgets

AI combat tools:
- shielding
- targeting
- healing or repair
- crowd control
- burst offense
- reconnaissance

Shared bond actions:
- combo finishers
- overdrive modes
- defensive link shields
- elemental fusion attacks
- emergency recovery bursts

## Progression

The progression model has three layers:

- Human growth: augmentations, weapon mastery, exploration, diplomacy, survivability
- AI growth: class evolution, protocol trees, elemental affinity, utility roles, personality alignment
- Bond growth: resonance level, combo unlocks, sync bonuses, ultimate abilities, trust events

## Multiplayer / Online Structure

- Shared hub cities with social and economic systems
- Instanced co-op expeditions for 2 to 4 players
- Regional public events with rotating faction pressure
- Persistent account progression and cloud-saved bonded roster
- Short, replayable pressure runs that fit a web-first service model
- Seasonal updates that add civilizations, anomalies, and narrative arcs

## Monetization Direction

Use a live-service model focused on expansion value rather than pay-to-win.

Recommended approach:
- free or low-cost entry
- optional seasonal pass
- cosmetic personalization for human and AI partners
- expansion zones and narrative chapters

Do not sell combat power directly.

## MVP Success Conditions

The first playable is successful if it proves:

1. The human + AI bond feels unique and strategically meaningful.
2. Co-op play improves the experience instead of adding chaos.
3. Progression creates attachment to both the player character and AI partner.
4. A single live event can change player goals and zone behavior in a noticeable way.
5. The AI partner reads as a co-agent rather than a passive bonus unit or collectible filler.
